How can we help?

Guides and troubleshooting for Preview studio, 2D plan view, room boundaries, and Live AR on iPhone and iPad.

Plan your room in Preview studio

Open Add furniture to choose from 12 offline 3D concept pieces, switch between Metric (cm / m) and Imperial (in / ft) under Layout options → Units, and set your room width and length under Room size to draw a floor boundary. Switch between the 2D plan and 3D orbit views in the header to check walkways and furniture clearances at a glance.

Adjust exact sizes, turns & locks

Tap any piece to drag it on the floor, pinch to resize, or rotate with two fingers. Open Adjust to enter exact width, height, or depth dimensions, turn in 45° or 90° increments, center a piece on the floor, or enable 5 cm grid snapping in Layout options. Tap the lock icon to lock foundational items such as rugs or large sofas against accidental movement.

Try Live AR in your room

Tap the viewfinder icon and allow camera access to place furniture on detected surfaces. Move your device slowly across a well-lit, textured floor until tracking is ready, then tap Place. Devices with a LiDAR Scanner add hardware depth occlusion; all AR and Metal rendering runs locally on your device.

Save, import & share

Your active layout, units preference, and grid setting save automatically in on-device storage. Use Layouts to store named arrangements and compare options. From Layout options, you can export a portable JSON layout file, a plain-text Dimensions list, or a snapshot image. Saved layouts reopen in Preview studio so you can inspect them anytime and place them again when starting Live AR.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera permission denied: Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and enable access for FurnishGrid, or continue designing completely offline in Preview studio and 2D plan view.
  • Piece will not move or scale: Check whether the lock icon is active on the piece or whether it would cross a set Room size boundary. Unlock the piece or expand the room size under Layout options.
  • Measurements & retail disclaimer: Furniture pieces in FurnishGrid are original generic concept models for visual planning. Real-world AR and camera tracking measurements are approximate—always verify physical dimensions before ordering furniture.

Contact support

Email admin@technoforbes.com with your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Share room snapshots or layout files only if you choose to include them.
