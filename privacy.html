Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

SpaceLoom is built to work privately on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect, sell, track, or upload your camera feed, room geometry, or furniture layouts.

No accounts, analytics, or servers

SpaceLoom requires no account or sign-in, contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not operate any remote servers that receive user data, telemetry, or diagnostics.

Camera & LiDAR depth data

Camera access is optional and requested only when you choose Start live AR. Live camera frames, plane detection results, environment probes, and LiDAR depth maps are processed strictly in device memory by Apple’s ARKit and Metal frameworks for real-time rendering and placement. They are never saved automatically or transmitted off your device. You can design in Preview studio and 2D plan view without granting camera access, or revoke camera permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Local layout storage

Your furniture arrangements, piece transforms, materials, unit preference, grid-snap setting, and optional room boundary dimensions are stored locally inside SpaceLoom’s protected Application Support storage on your device. If your device backs up data through iCloud or Finder according to your system settings, Apple manages those backups under your iOS settings. You can delete saved layouts in Layouts, clear your current workspace via New layout, or remove all app data by deleting SpaceLoom from your device.

Exporting & sharing

When you choose Share image, Share layout file, or Share dimensions list, SpaceLoom prepares a temporary export only for that share sheet action and deletes the temporary export folder when sharing finishes. A shared snapshot image may show your room if taken in Live AR. The app or destination you choose in the iOS Share Sheet receives that exported content under its own terms and privacy policy. If you choose Save Image inside the iOS Share Sheet, iOS may request write-only permission to add the image to Photos. SpaceLoom does not browse your photo library, contacts, microphone, or location.
